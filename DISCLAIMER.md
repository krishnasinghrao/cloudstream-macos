# Legal Disclaimer & DMCA Policy

## 1. General Disclaimer

**CloudStream for macOS** is a free, open-source media player, workstation, and indexing framework.

* **No Media Hosted:** CloudStream for macOS does not host, store, stream, or distribute any media files, video streams, or copyright-protected content.
* **No Built-in Scrapers:** The application ships without built-in video providers or scraper repositories. By default, the application cannot stream or index third-party video content.
* **Neutral Tool:** Like web browsers, torrent clients, or media players (such as VLC or Kodi), CloudStream for macOS is a neutral software tool.

## 2. Third-Party Community Extensions

* **Independent Development:** CloudStream for macOS supports an extensible plugin system compatible with community-maintained `.cs3` extensions. These extensions are developed, maintained, and hosted by independent, third-party developers on separate platforms (such as GitHub, GitLab, Codeberg, or personal servers).
* **Zero Endorsement or Control:** The maintainers of CloudStream for macOS do not endorse, control, moderate, review, or maintain third-party extension repositories. We have no involvement in the creation, maintenance, or data feeds of external extensions.
* **User Responsibility:** Users install third-party extension repositories at their own sole discretion and responsibility. Users are obligated to comply with the copyright and intellectual property laws of their respective jurisdictions.
