# Watching live channels

A live channel shows **Live** above the timeline. Opening one joins it at its
live position: a position saved for a film is never applied to a channel.

## The timeline

While you follow the broadcast the timeline stays at **Live**. Pausing or
seeking back puts it in **Replay**; **Go Live** returns to the broadcast by
opening the stream again.

You can seek only inside what the player has already downloaded, which is the
part of the timeline that is drawn. With nothing downloaded yet the seek
buttons are off. The timeline does not move under the pointer while you drag,
even as new video arrives.

## When a channel stops

If the stream fails, ends unexpectedly or stops loading, the player says
*Reconnecting to the live stream…* and tries again for up to 45 seconds: first
with fresh links from the provider, then with another source if the title has
one. If the channel stays away you get an error with **Retry**.

Pausing stops the reconnect. It starts again when you press play.

A broadcast that has really finished closes the player, like the end of a film.

## History

A channel you watched appears in History, without a watched percentage. It is
not added to Continue Watching and has no resume point.
